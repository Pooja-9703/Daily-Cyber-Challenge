# **Assignment \#3 – Network Interfaces**

## **THEORY**

**1\. What is a Network Interface Card?**  
      A Network Interface Card (NIC) is a hardware component that allows a computer or device to connect to a network. It can provide either wired Ethernet or wireless connectivity.

**2\. What is Ethernet?**  
      Ethernet is a widely used networking technology for connecting devices over a wired network. It defines how data is transmitted between devices on a LAN.

**3\. What is RJ-45?**  
      RJ-45 is the commonly used connector for Ethernet network cables. It has eight contacts and is used with twisted-pair Ethernet cables.

**4\. What does UTP stand for?**  
      UTP stands for Unshielded Twisted Pair. It consists of pairs of copper wires twisted together to reduce electromagnetic interference.

**5\. Name three common Ethernet cable categories.**  
      Three common Ethernet cable categories are:

    * Cat5e  
    * Cat6  
    * Cat6a

**6\. What is a straight-through cable?**  
      A straight-through cable has the same wiring standard at both ends, such as T568A-to-T568A or T568B-to-T568B. It is commonly used to connect different types of devices, such as a computer to a switch.

**7\. What is a crossover cable?**  
      A crossover cable uses different wiring standards at each end, such as T568A on one end and T568B on the other. It was traditionally used to connect similar devices directly, such as computer-to-computer.

**8\. Explain Auto MDI-X.**  
      Auto MDI-X is a feature that automatically detects the type of Ethernet connection and adjusts the transmit and receive pairs as needed. This allows devices to communicate without requiring a specific straight-through or crossover cable.

**9\. Difference between copper and fiber-optic networking?**  
      Copper networking uses electrical signals transmitted through copper cables, while fiber-optic networking uses light signals transmitted through optical fiber. Fiber generally supports longer distances, higher bandwidth, and better resistance to electromagnetic interference.

**10\. What is multimode fiber?**  
       Multimode fiber (MMF) is an optical fiber that allows multiple light paths or modes to travel through the core. It is commonly used for shorter-distance networking, such as within buildings and data centers.

**11\. What is single-mode fiber?**  
       Single-mode fiber (SMF) is an optical fiber with a small core that allows primarily one mode of light to travel through it. It is suitable for long-distance communication and high-bandwidth applications.

**12\. Two advantages of fiber-optic networking.**  
       Two advantages of fiber-optic networking are:

    * **High bandwidth** – It can carry large amounts of data at high speeds.  
    * **Long transmission distance** – It can transmit data over much longer    distances than typical copper Ethernet without significant signal loss.

**13\. Why is understanding network interfaces important for cybersecurity?**  
       Understanding network interfaces is important for cybersecurity because security professionals need to know how devices connect to networks and how network traffic enters and leaves systems. This knowledge helps with network monitoring, traffic analysis, identifying unauthorized connections, troubleshooting, and investigating security incidents.
